# YouTube [Paid Service Usage Rules](https://www.youtube.com/t/usage_paycontent) Analysis

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Document** | YouTube Paid Service Usage Rules |
| **My Assessment** | A set of restrictive rules that govern how I can access and use content I have paid for. These rules are designed to limit my usage, not to provide me with flexibility. They are a continuation of the user-hostile pattern I have documented across all major platforms |

## 2. My Critical Findings

### 2.1. Rental Content: Limited Viewing Period (Section: "Rental Content")

The rules state: *"When you rent content, you may view such item of content an unlimited number of times during the period of time displayed at the time of your payment ("Viewing Period")... Pausing, stopping, or rewinding an item of rented content will not extend your applicable Viewing Period."*

**My Concern:** I am paying for temporary access. The clock continues ticking even if I pause, stop, or rewind. This is a punitive restriction. If I have to pause a movie for an emergency, I lose that time. This isn't a customer-friendly policy, it's designed to maximize the likelihood that I won't fully use what I paid for.

### 2.2. Purchased Content: Locker Period (Section: "Purchased Content")

The rules state: *"The 'Locker Period' will be for at least 5 years from the date of your purchase... if an item of purchased content becomes unavailable during the five year period from the purchase date, you may request a refund."*

**My Concern:** I don't own content I purchase on YouTube. I am buying a license that can be revoked. The "Locker Period" is a limited window of access. After five years, YouTube can remove the content and offer only a "partial refund" at their discretion. I am paying for a product that can be taken away from me. This isn't ownership, it's a long-term rental.

### 2.3. Purchased Content: Streaming Limitations (Section: "Purchased Content")

The rules state:

- *"you may view one stream of each item at a time,"*
- *"you may view up to 3 streams of different items at a time,"*
- *"you may authorize up to 5 devices for offline playback... and to authorize additional devices, you must deauthorize one of those 5 devices,"*
- *"you may only authorize the same device three times in any 12 month period and de-authorize the same device twice in any 12 month period,"*
- *"you may only deauthorize a total of 2 devices for offline playback every 90 days,"*
- *"you may only authorize 3 Google accounts on the same device."*

**My Concern:** These are strict, arbitrary limits. I cannot watch the same content on multiple devices simultaneously. I can only authorize a limited number of devices for offline playback. I can only authorize or deauthorize devices a limited number of times per year. This isn't designed for user convenience, it's designed to restrict usage and prevent sharing. I am being treated as a potential pirate, not a customer.

### 2.4. Geographic Restrictions (Section: "General")

The rules state: *"Due to restrictions from our content providers, purchased or rented content may not be available for purchase in all countries or outside of your home country."*

**My Concern:** I cannot access content I paid for if I travel outside my home country. This is a punitive restriction that limits how I can use a service I have paid for. It's a reminder that I don't truly own the content.
