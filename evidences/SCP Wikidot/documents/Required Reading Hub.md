## 8. Conclusion

The Required Reading Hub is a well-organized collection of essential documents. It sets high expectations for all users, especially authors, and makes it clear that ignorance of the rules isn't an excuse.

**Key takeaway:** The SCP Wikidot isn't a casual platform. It requires effort, education, and compliance.
