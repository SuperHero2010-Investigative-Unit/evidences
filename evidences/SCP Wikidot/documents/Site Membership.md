## 2. Key Requirements

### 2.1. Age Requirement

> *"You must be at least 18. This website contains mature themes and discussions, and we expect members to behave in a mature and adult-like manner. All members are expected to be at least 18 years old, and to have reached the age of majority within the nations of which they live. There are no exceptions to this rule, ever."*

| Implication | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **18+ only** | No exceptions |
| **Mature content** | The site contains adult themes |

### 2.2. Account Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **One account per person** | No alts, no account sharing |
| **Username policy** | Strict rules on usernames |
| **Email restrictions** | Avoid numbers, Outlook, or Hotmail |

### 2.3. Username Policy

| Prohibited | Allowed |
|------------|---------|
| Profanity, slurs, offensive content | Generic names |
| Self-degrading names (e.g., "iamastupididiot") | Names referencing non-SCP characters |
| Names implying staff positions | Names like "Agent Anders" |
| Names based on pre-established Foundation characters | Names not impersonating notable figures |
| Impersonating notable public figures | |

### 2.4. Application Process

> *"You're expected to include the sentence, 'I will follow site rules and respect community members; I acknowledge that submitting AI-assisted/generated content will result in a permanent ban.' Input that sentence, nothing else."*

| Implication | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **Mandatory statement** | Must be included exactly |
| **AI ban acknowledgment** | Explicit acknowledgment of AI rules |
| **Nothing else** | Only the sentence should be entered |

## 8. Conclusion

The "Join the Site" page reveals a strict, structured membership process. Applicants must:

1. Be 18 or older
2. Follow strict username rules
3. Acknowledge the AI ban
4. Agree to follow site rules

**Key takeaway:** The SCP Wikidot isn't a casual platform. It has significant requirements for membership, and violations can result in permanent bans.
