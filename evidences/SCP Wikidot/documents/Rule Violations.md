# Rule Violations

## 1. Overview

The SCP Wikidot has a comprehensive set of rules. These rules are strictly enforced. Violations result in rejection, deletion, or permanent bans.

This document lists the rules and the violations that result in rejection.

---

## 2. Object Class Violations

| Violation | Rule | Consequence |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **Custom Object Classes** | Only approved classes are allowed | Article rejected and deleted |
| **Non-standard classes** | Safe, Euclid, Keter, Thaumiel, Apollyon, Archon, Argus, Cernunnos, Ticonderoga, Explained, Neutralized, Decommissioned, Pending, Uncontained | Article rejected |
| **Explained without -EX** | Explained SCPs must use `-EX` suffix | Article rejected |
| **Incorrect class** | Class must match the anomaly | Article rejected |

---

## 3. Content Violations

| Violation | Rule | Consequence |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **AI-generated content** | Generative AI is banned | Permanent ban |
| **Plagiarism** | Copying others' work is prohibited | Deletion and possible ban |
| **Low-quality articles** | Staff may ask you to stop posting | Deletion |
| **Blank or incomplete pages** | Pages must be complete | Deletion |
| **Unedited reposts** | Reposts must be edited | Deletion |
| **Trolling** | Malicious posting is prohibited | Permanent ban |
| **Harassment** | Harassment is prohibited | Permanent ban |
| **Threats of legal action** | Threats against the wiki are prohibited | Permanent ban |

---

## 4. Behavior Violations

| Violation | Rule | Consequence |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **Rule Zero violation** | Don't be a dick | Warning to permanent ban |
| **Brigading** | Calling for group voting is prohibited | Ban |
| **Spam** | Contentless posts are prohibited | Warning to ban |
| **Double posting** | Edit previous posts instead | Warning |
| **Necroposting** | Don't post on old threads | Warning |
| **Hate speech** | Slurs and hate speech are prohibited | Permanent ban |

---

## 5. Staff Interaction Violations

| Violation | Rule | Consequence |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **Disobeying staff** | Staff requests must be complied with | Warning to ban |
| **Continuing stopped discussions** | Stop orders must be respected | Warning to ban |
| **Appealing to multiple staff** | Appeals must go through proper channels | Warning |

---

## 6. Offsite Behavior Violations

| Violation | Rule | Consequence |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **Offsite misconduct** | Offsite behavior can affect standing | Warning to ban |
| **Raiding other sites** | Organizing raids is prohibited | Permanent ban |
| **Adversarial community membership** | Membership in adversarial communities | Application denied or membership revoked |

---

## 7. Internal Foundation War Violations

| Violation | Rule | Consequence |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **Internal Foundation war** | Foundation is unified | Article rejected |
| **Military opposition** | Military obeys O5 | Article rejected |
| **O5 defiance** | O5 Council is supreme | Article rejected |
| **SCP intervention in Foundation affairs** | SCPs are contained | Article rejected |

---

## 8. Summary of Consequences

| Consequence | Trigger |
|-------------|---------|
| **Warning** | Minor violations |
| **Membership Revocation** | Repeated violations |
| **Short Ban (week to month)** | Serious violations |
| **Long Ban (month to year)** | Severe violations |
| **Permanent Ban** | Extreme violations (AI, plagiarism, harassment, threats) |
| **Article Deletion** | Rule violations, low score, custom classes |

---

## 9. Conclusion

The SCP Wikidot has strict rules. Violations result in rejection, deletion, or bans. The rules are enforced without mercy. There are no exceptions.

This isn't about quality.
This is about control.
