# The Illusion of Power

## 1. Overview

This document analyzes the fundamental difference between community-driven platforms (like the SCP Wikidot) and owner-driven canons (like The Outrooms). It exposes the "illusion of power" that communities believe they hold, and how that illusion is shattered when the original creator returns.

---

## 2. The Illusion

The community of the SCP Wikidot lives in a bubble of perceived authority. They believe their votes matter. They believe their downvotes control the canon. They believe they are the ones who decide what is acceptable and what is rejected.

This illusion works because the creator is silent. The creator built the platform, stepped back, and let the community run wild. The community filled the vacuum. They mistook the creator's silence for permission.

| Element | The Illusion |
|---------|--------------|
| **The Community** | Believes it controls the canon |
| **The Votes** | Believes they determine what is accepted |
| **The Downvotes** | Believes they determine what is rejected |
| **The Power** | Believes it's real |

---

## 3. The Shattering

The illusion cannot last forever. It's a fragile thing. It survives only because the original creator is absent.

If the original creator (Moto42) returned and created his own wiki, the community would flock to it. They would assume they have the same power there as they do on the SCP Wikidot. They would try to downvote. They would try to enforce their consensus.

And then, they would be told: **No.**

The original author would say: *"I built my first SCP. I built the foundation. You built your Wikidot based on my foundation. You were just extending my SCP universe. But your extension is fanmade. Mine is official. I control everything, not you. You're just having an illusion of power. If I didn't publish my first SCP article, SCP Wikidot wouldn't exist, you wouldn't exist. Without me, you're nothingness."*

The illusion would shatter in an instant.
