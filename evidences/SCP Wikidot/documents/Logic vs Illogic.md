# Logic vs Illogic

## 1. Overview

The SCP Wikidot claims to be a logical, curated platform. It claims to value quality, consistency, and creativity. It claims to be open and welcoming.

This document examines whether these claims are logical.

---

## 2. The Wiki's Claims

| Claim | Stated Purpose |
|-------|----------------|
| **Logical** | The wiki is based on reason and consistency |
| **Quality-driven** | The wiki values high-quality writing |
| **Creative** | The wiki encourages creativity |
| **Open** | The wiki is welcoming to new contributors |
| **Fair** | The wiki applies rules consistently |

---

## 3. The Reality

| Claim | Reality | Logical? |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Logical** | Custom classes are prohibited even when they fit | Illogical |
| **Quality-driven** | Deletion at -10, regardless of quality | Illogical |
| **Creative** | -EX suffix is mandatory, even when redundant | Illogical |
| **Open** | 18+, strict username, AI ban | Illogical |
| **Fair** | Staff discretion, subjective enforcement | Illogical |

---

## 4. Examples of Illogic

| Example | Why It's Illogical |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Custom classes prohibited** | If a class fits the anomaly, why prohibit it? |
| **-EX suffix mandatory** | If the class says "Explained," why add -EX? |
| **Deletion at -10** | If an article is good, why delete it for a score? |
| **AI ban absolute** | If AI helps writing, why ban it completely? |
| **18+ only** | If the content is mature, why not rate it? |
| **Staff discretion** | If rules are clear, why are they subjective? |

---

## 5. The Definition of Logical

| Aspect | Logical | Illogical |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Consistency** | Rules apply equally | Rules apply unevenly |
| **Clarity** | Rules are clear | Rules are vague |
| **Purpose** | Rules serve a purpose | Rules serve control |
| **Flexibility** | Rules allow exceptions | Rules are rigid |
| **Reason** | Rules are based on reason | Rules are based on tradition |

---

## 6. The Wiki's Illogic

| Illogic | Explanation |
|---------|-------------|
| **Rigid rules** | No exceptions, no flexibility |
| **Vague rules** | "Don't be a dick" is subjective |
| **Redundant rules** | -EX suffix adds nothing |
| **Control-focused rules** | Custom classes prohibited to maintain control |
| **Fear-based rules** | AI ban is an overreaction |

---

## 7. Conclusion

The SCP Wikidot claims to be logical. It isn't.

It claims to value quality. It values control.
It claims to encourage creativity. It prohibits it.
It claims to be open. It's closed.
It claims to be fair. It's subjective.

The wiki isn't logical.
The wiki is illogical.
