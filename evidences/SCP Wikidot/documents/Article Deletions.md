## 2. Reasons for Deletion

### 2.1. Low Score

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Score ≤ -10** | Staff posts a 24-hour timer |
| **Two additional staff witnesses required** | Three staff members total |
| **Score reaches -20 or lower** | Deletion occurs immediately |
| **Score rises to -8 or higher** | Deletion is canceled |

> *"If a page’s score falls to -10 or lower, an eligible member of staff will post a 24-hour timer in the article's comments. Two additional staff witnesses are required for deletion."*

**Exemptions from score-based deletion:**

| Tag | Pages with this tag are exempt |
|-----|-------------------------------|
| `admin` | Staff pages |
| `author` | Author pages |
| `guide` | Guides |
| `more-by` | More-by pages |
| `redirect` | Redirect pages |
| `sandbox` | Draft pages |
| `workbench` | Workbench pages |

### 2.2. Rule Violations

Articles that violate site rules or policies may be deleted immediately. This includes:

| Violation | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| **Blank or incomplete pages** | Empty or unfinished articles |
| **Unedited reposts** | Reposting without changes |
| **Plagiarism** | Copying others' work |
| **AI-generated content** | ChatGPT, Midjourney, etc. |
| **Trolling** | Malicious posting |
| **Unauthorized pages** | Pages posted by banned users |
| **Legal risks** | Content that could lead to legal action |
| **Security risks** | Content that threatens site security |
| **Custom Object Classes** | Use of non-standard Object Classes is prohibited. Articles using custom classes will be deleted |

### 2.3. Cleanup and Special Events

| Reason | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Staff cleanup** | Pages created for official duties may be deleted |
| **Contest rules** | Event pages may have additional deletion conditions |

## 3. Self-Deletion Process

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| **1** | Click "Options" at the bottom of the page |
| **2** | Click "Delete" |
| **3** | Select "Delete Completely" |
| **4** | Announce deletion in the Deletions thread |

> *"Be sure to select 'Delete Completely,' as a failure to do this does not remove the page; it simply renames it to '/deleted:page-url'."*

---

## 4. Key Policies

| Policy | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Deletion is never a punishment** | Pages are deleted for content reasons, not as punishment |
| **Timer exceptions** | Only used for low score and failure to credit coauthors |
| **Staff witnesses** | Minimum 3 staff members required |
| **Author notification** | Staff should contact authors when changes are made |

---

## 5. Questions and Answers

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **How many staff witnesses?** | At least 3 |
| **When are deletion timers used?** | Only for low score and coauthor issues |
| **My article is at -10. Should I delete it?** | Yes. It rarely recovers. |
| **Can I get a copy of my deleted article?** | Staff will try to include the source, but it's not guaranteed. |
| **Will staff delete my page for me?** | No. You must delete it yourself. |

## 9. Conclusion

The Deletions Guide reveals a system where articles are easily removed. A score of -10 triggers deletion. Staff can delete for rule violations. Authors are encouraged to delete their own work.

**Key takeaway:** The SCP Wikidot operates on a "delete first" model. Articles are disposable, and authors must constantly monitor their scores to avoid deletion.
