## 2. Key Rules

### Rule 1: Critique the work, not the author

| Prohibited | Recommended |
|------------|-------------|
| "You're a terrible writer" | "This needs work. The main idea's fine except for the X, but addenda are complete crap." |
| Personal attacks | Stick to criticizing the writing |

> *"Do not make personal attacks on the author. Stick to criticizing their writing."*

### Rule 2: Posts must contain content

| Prohibited | Recommended |
|------------|-------------|
| "Meh" | Meaningful feedback |
| Emoticons alone | Suggestions for improvement |
| "lol" | Specific critiques |

> *"Only saying 'Meh' does not count as content, because it doesn't tell the author or other readers any information at all."*

### Rule 3: Don't be vague

| Prohibited | Recommended |
|------------|-------------|
| "The problems with this article should be obvious" | Specific explanations of issues |

> *"If they were obvious to the author, they wouldn't have made them, and a post like that contains no content except obliquely insulting the author."*

---

## 3. Guidelines

### 3.1. Giving Critique

| Guideline | Explanation |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Be helpful** | Suggest what the author did wrong and how to improve |
| **Be harsh if needed** | But harsh ≠ mean |
| **Be specific** | Forum critique should be in-depth |
| **Don't dog-pile** | If others have already covered the flaws, add something new |
| **Keep an open mind** | Respect stylistic choices |

### 3.2. In Short

> *"Be helpful, civil, and open-minded."*

## 8. Conclusion

The Criticism Policy is designed to foster a productive critique culture. It encourages helpful, specific feedback while discouraging personal attacks, vagueness, and dog-piling. It's enforced by staff at their discretion.

**Key takeaway:** The SCP Wikidot expects constructive criticism. Authors and reviewers are both held to standards of civility and helpfulness.
