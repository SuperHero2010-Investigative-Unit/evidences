# Case 12: SCP Wikidot

---

## Case Overview

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Target** | SCP Foundation – SCP Wikidot |
| **Version** | Any version |
| **Investigation Period** | August 30, 2026 – August 31, 2026 |
| **Status** | Published |
| **Charges** | Strict rules that stifle creativity, non-standard classes rejected, -EX suffix required, inconsistent enforcement, internal conflicts, lack of transparency |

---

## Summary of Findings

| # | Finding | Description |
|---|---------|-------------|
| 1 | **Non-standard classes prohibited** | The SCP Wikidot doesn't allow custom object classes like "Neutralized (De Facto)". Only Safe, Euclid, Keter, Thaumiel, Neutralized, Explained |
| 2 | **-EX suffix required** | Explained-class SCPs must have the `-EX` suffix. Omitting it results in rejection |
| 3 | **Deletion at -10** | Articles with a score of -10 are deleted. Staff can also delete for rule violations |
| 4 | **18+ only** | Membership requires age 18+. No exceptions |
| 5 | **AI ban is absolute** | AI-generated content results in a permanent ban |
| 6 | **Staff discretion** | Rules are subjective and enforced by staff |
| 7 | **High barrier to entry** | Multiple requirements for membership (age, username, AI acknowledgment) |
| 8 | **Deletion culture** | Articles are disposable, self-deletion is expected |
| 9 | **Rights Agent Notice** | Users authorize staff to act as rights agents |
| 10 | **Brutal enforcement** | Rules are enforced without mercy |

## Comparison with Marketing Claims

| Claim | Reality | Discrepancy |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| "Anyone can contribute" | Contributors face strict rules, rejection, and deletion | False |
| "Community-driven" | Staff have final authority, community voting can be overridden | False |
| "Creative freedom" | Creativity is limited by strict rules and formatting requirements | False |
| "Transparent" | Staff decisions are often not clearly communicated | False |

---

## Conclusion

> *"The SCP Wikidot markets itself as a community-driven platform for creative writing. However, its strict rules, inconsistent enforcement, and lack of transparency create a system that stifles creativity and discourages new contributors. The deletion culture reveals internal conflicts and power imbalances. The SCP Wikidot isn't the open, creative community it claims to be."*
