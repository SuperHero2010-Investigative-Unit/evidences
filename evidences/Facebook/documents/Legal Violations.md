## Detailed Violations

### 1. Unconscionable Contract Terms

| Clause | Violation | Evidence |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Broad License to User Content** | Grants Meta a worldwide, perpetual, transferable license to user content | Facebook Terms of Service (Section 3.3) |
| **Use of Name and Likeness for Ads** | Users grant Meta permission to use their name and likeness for advertising without compensation | Facebook Terms of Service (Section 3.3) |
| **Liability Cap** | Meta disclaims liability for all damages | Facebook Terms of Service (Section 4.3) |
| **Indemnification Clause** | Users must defend Meta against claims | Facebook Terms of Service |

**Legal Basis:** U.S. contract law (unconscionability), EU Unfair Contract Terms Directive, UK Unfair Contract Terms Act.

### 2. Violation of Privacy Rights

| Clause | Violation | Evidence |
|--------|-----------|----------|
