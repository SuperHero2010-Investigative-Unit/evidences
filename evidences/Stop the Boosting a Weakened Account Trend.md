# Stop the Boosting a Weakened Account Trend

## Overview

I'm writing this document because I've witnessed a trend that has spread across social media platforms. The trend is called "Boosting a Weakened Account". It's a scam. It's viewbait. It's fraud. It promises free followers, free verified followers, free views, and free money. It delivers nothing. This must stop.

---

## What the Trend Is

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Boosting a weakened account | A trend where users promise to boost followers, views, or money |
| Viewbait | Posts designed to generate views, not deliver promises |
| Engagement farming | Posts designed to generate comments, not deliver promises |
| Scam | Fraudulent promises, no delivery |
