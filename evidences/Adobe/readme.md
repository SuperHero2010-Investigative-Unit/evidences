## Key Findings

### 1. Pricing is Inconsistent and Deceptive

| Example | My Observation |
|---------|----------------|
| Photography ($19.99) cheaper than Lightroom ($11.99) + Photoshop ($22.99) individually | Bundle pricing is designed to confuse and push users toward higher-cost plans |
| Substance 3D Texturing ($24.99) cheaper than Substance 3D Collection ($59.99) | Users are hooked on cheaper plans then upsold to expensive collections |

### 2. Subscription Terms are Traps

| Clause | My Concern |
|--------|------------|
| Automatic renewal without notice | Users must remember to cancel or be charged |
| 50% early termination fee | Punitive barrier to leaving |
| No refund after 14 days | Forces users to stay locked in |

### 3. Legal Terms Are One-Sided

| Clause | My Concern |
|--------|------------|
| Broad license to user content | Adobe can use my work for multiple purposes |
| $100 liability cap | No real protection for users |
| Mandatory arbitration | Removes right to sue or join class-action lawsuits |
| Indemnification | Users must defend Adobe against claims |

### 4. Enforcement is Aggressive and Selective

| Observation | Implication |
|-------------|-------------|
| Legitimate users are locked out | AGS is flawed and punitive |
| Older versions are ignored | Enforcement only targets latest versions |
| Users forced to third-party sites | Adobe creates security risks for users |
