## Detailed Violations

### 1. Unconscionable Contract Terms

| Clause | Violation | Evidence |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Broad License to User Content** | Grants Adobe a broad, worldwide license to user content | General Terms of Use (Section 4.3) |
| **$100 Liability Cap** | Limits liability to a token amount | General Terms of Use (Section 10) |
| **Indemnification Clause** | Users must defend Adobe against claims | General Terms of Use (Section 8.2) |
| **Mandatory Arbitration** | Removes right to sue or join class action | General Terms of Use (Section 14) |

**Legal Basis:** U.S. contract law (unconscionability), EU Unfair Contract Terms Directive.

---

### 2. Violation of Privacy Rights

| Clause | Violation | Evidence |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Vast Data Collection** | Collects excessive personal data without meaningful consent | Privacy Policy |
| **Biometric Data** | Collects biometric information without proper consent | Privacy Policy |
| **No Real Opt-Out** | Users can't opt out of data collection | Privacy Policy |
| **Data Sharing** | Shares data with third parties without explicit consent | Privacy Policy |
