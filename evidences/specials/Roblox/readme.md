## Key Findings

### Forced Migration Without Consent

Users with Vietnamese IP addresses are automatically migrated to Roblox VNG. This migration isn't optional. Users report being locked into the VNG region without consent. One user from Russia reported: "I haven't even been to Vietnam, nor logged in from Vietnam". Another user stated: "I'm not even living in Vietnam. I haven't used any VPN or DNS proxy".

### The Region Lock Trap

Once migrated, users are stuck. They can't access the global Roblox version. They can't change their region back. The VNG app is region-locked to Vietnamese app stores, so international users can't even download it to comply with the forced migration.

### Support Failures: AI Bots and Ignored Tickets

Roblox VNG support is widely criticized as useless. Users report receiving identical AI-generated responses regardless of what they write. Tickets are ignored for months. One user waited over a month with no response.

### Arbitrary Restrictions

Roblox VNG enforces restrictions that don't exist on the global version. Chat and voice chat locked even for age-verified users. Screen time limits of 1 to 3 hours daily, even for adults. Popular games removed, "harmful" games kept. VPNs blocked, region changes prohibited. Robux priced higher than global. Roblox Plus unavailable.

### The Birthday Lock and Age Guessing

Roblox VNG doesn't allow users to change their birthday. There is no user-facing option to correct it. Instead, the platform estimates age automatically, without user input. Users can't fix an incorrect birthday. They can't challenge the guessed age.

### Roblox Select: The New Wall

Roblox Select is a Vietnamese version of Roblox that creates a walled garden for users. It separates them from the global platform, blocks access to Roblox Global, and traps them inside. Users are assigned to Select mode without consent. One user reported: "All my accounts are assigned to Select mode. No one asked me. No one warned me. No one gave me a choice".

### The Korea Parallel

North Korea and South Korea are divided by a wall. Roblox VNG and Roblox Global are divided by a region lock. The parallel is exact. The same control. The same trap. The same outcome.
