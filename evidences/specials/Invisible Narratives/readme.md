## Key Findings

### The Collaboration

One year ago, Alexey Gerasimov voluntarily collaborated with Invisible Narratives to fight other companies trying to attack Dafuq!?Boom!. Initially, the company performed properly what he had asked.

### The Contract Trap

After about months, Invisible Narratives sent him a contract and asked him to sign. As expected, Alexey Gerasimov signed. He lost the channel. The root channel was changed to "Skibidi".

### The Betrayal

After the contract, Invisible Narratives posted videos made of Unreal Engine. Strider betrayed the Skibidi Toilet community, doing Episode 80, Season 26, and Episode 81. These videos are too bad. Users criticize intensely.

### The Anger

Alexey Gerasimov changed his profile image on his X account to an angry face. Strider imitated. After Episode 80 published, he followed Law by Mike, a lawyer who made a contract to call him to join. After Episode 81, he changed the cover into the face that was angry with the words "R U" in left glass part and " FR?" in right glass part.

### The Meaning of "R U FR?"

"R U FR?" means "Are you for real?" This is a direct message to Invisible Narratives and Strider.

### Law by Mike

Law by Mike publicly offered to help Gerasimov. In a video, he addressed Gerasimov: "Alexey, if you're watching this, you may have signed something that prevents you from publicly discussing aspects of your contract. But I got good news for you. No one can keep you from talking to a lawyer. Reach out to me. We can see if there's a legal way to help."

### The Copyright Strikes

After the YouTubers' open letter, Invisible Narratives hit 10+ Skibidi Toilet YouTube channels with copyright strikes. ZacDoor's channel risked being permanently deleted by YouTube. Invisible Narratives later retracted all strikes after a manual review within 24 hours.

### The Federal Lawsuit

Invisible Narratives sued Next Level Apps for copyright infringement. The court granted an injunction preventing Next Level from issuing takedown notices.
