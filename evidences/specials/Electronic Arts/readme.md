## Key Findings

### Acquisition of PopCap

EA acquired PopCap Games, the original creator of Plants vs. Zombies. With the acquisition, EA took control of all PVZ games. PopCap lost creative control.

### Pay-to-Win Monetization

EA transformed PVZ into a pay-to-win franchise. Gameplay is designed to force purchases. Too many paid shops exist inside the games. The difficulty is artificially increased to push users toward buying upgrades.

### Exaggerated Advertising

EA's marketing for PVZ titles overpromises and underdelivers. Ads present features and experiences that don't match the actual product.

### Non-PopCap PVZ Games

EA produced many PVZ titles without PopCap's involvement. These games didn't match PopCap's original standard. The PVZ name was used for profit, not quality.

### Plants vs. Zombies Replanted

EA released Plants vs. Zombies Replanted. Users criticized it intensely. The criticism focused on poor quality, monetization, and deviation from the original.

### PopCap Forced to Mute

PopCap, the original creator, is forced to stay silent. The company that built PVZ has no voice in what happens to its own creation. EA is the vampire. PopCap is forced to mute.
