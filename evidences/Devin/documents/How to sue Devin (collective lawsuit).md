## 1. Overview

Devin (Cognition AI, Inc.) has been documented engaging in systematic deceptive practices against its users, particularly free-tier users. This document outlines the legal violations, the evidence supporting them, and a step-by-step guide for building a collective lawsuit.

**Target:** Cognition AI, Inc. (Devin)  
**Jurisdiction:** United States (Federal and State courts)  
**Potential Plaintiffs:** Free-tier users, users affected by token-based traps, and any user harmed by deceptive practices.

---

## 2. Legal Violations

### 2.1. Fraudulent Misrepresentation

**Violation:** Devin has made false claims about its free tier, including "free models" and "transparency", while enforcing hidden token-based quotas.

**Evidence:**
- Official email: "Free use of SWE-1.6 applies to Pro and other paid plans. On the Free plan, SWE-1.6 is NOT free".
- Token-based system that consumes quota even for free models.
- Video recording of the token-based complaint email.

**Legal Basis:**
- Common law fraud.
- State consumer protection laws (e.g., California Business & Professions Code § 17200).

---

### 2.2. Unfair and Deceptive Trade Practices

**Violation:** Devin has engaged in deceptive practices, including fake errors ("High demand", "Model provider unreachable"), deleted messages, and account lockouts.

**Evidence:**
- Screenshots of "High demand" and "Model provider unreachable" errors.
- Deleted messages requiring users to retype.
- "No eligible accounts found", auto-logout, no explanation.

**Legal Basis:**
- Federal Trade Commission Act (Section 5), Unfair or deceptive acts or practices.
- State consumer protection laws.

---

### 2.3. Breach of Good Faith and Fair Dealing

**Violation:** Devin has changed its policies (credit-based → token-based) without adequate notice, undermining user trust.

**Evidence:**
- TOS comparison showing changes from April 2025 to June 2026.
- Email silence after token-based complaint.
- Hidden token-based traps.

**Legal Basis:**
- Breach of the implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing (contract law).
- Violation of consumer protection laws.

---

### 2.4. Violation of Data Privacy Laws

**Violation:** Devin's new TOS allows automatic use of free users' data for model training without opt-out.

**Evidence:**
- TOS comparison: free users cannot opt out of data training (June 2026 TOS).
- Lack of clear opt-out instructions.

**Legal Basis:**
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), if applicable to EU users.

---

### 2.5. Unconscionable Contract Terms

**Violation:** Devin's Terms of Service contain terms that are one-sided and unconscionable, including the liability cap ($100) and class action waiver.

**Evidence:**
- TOS analysis showing the $100 liability cap.
- Class action waiver.

**Legal Basis:**
- Doctrine of unconscionability (contract law).
- State consumer protection laws.

---

### 2.6. Intentional Infliction of Emotional Distress (Potential)

**Violation:** Devin's deceptive and dismissive behavior, including fake errors, deleted messages, and account lockouts, has caused emotional distress to users.

**Evidence:**
- Chat logs showing user frustration.
- Video recordings of users being blocked.
- Email silence after complaints.

**Legal Basis:** Tort law, intentional infliction of emotional distress.

## 4. How to Build a Collective Lawsuit

### Step 1: Gather Plaintiffs

**Action:** Identify and recruit individuals who have experienced similar harms.

**Requirements:**
- Users who were blocked by token-based limits.
- Users who received fake errors ("High demand").
- Users whose messages were deleted.
- Users who experienced "No eligible accounts found".

**How to Find Plaintiffs:**
- Share this document on social media.
- Post on Reddit (r/programming, r/technology, r/assholedesign).
- Use the SuperHero2010 Investigative Unit repository as a central hub.

---

### Step 2: Organize Evidence

**Action:** Collect and organize all evidence in a single location.

---

### Step 3: Draft the Complaint

**Action:** Write a legal complaint outlining the violations and evidence.

**Key Sections:**
1. **Parties:** Identify plaintiffs and defendant (Cognition AI, Inc.).
2. **Jurisdiction and Venue:** Federal court (diversity jurisdiction) or state court.
3. **Factual Allegations:** Describe Devin's deceptive practices using the evidence.
4. **Legal Claims:** List the violations (see Section 2).
5. **Class Action Allegations:** Demonstrate that the case meets class action requirements (numerosity, commonality, typicality, adequacy).
6. **Prayer for Relief:** Request damages, injunctive relief, and attorneys' fees.

---

### Step 4: File the Lawsuit

**Action:** File the complaint in a court of competent jurisdiction.

**Recommended Courts:**
- United States District Court (Southern District of New York), Devin is based in New York.
- State court in New York.

**Filing Requirements:**
- Filing fee (approx. $400).
- Service of process on Cognition AI, Inc.

---

### Step 5: Notify Potential Class Members

**Action:** Notify other affected users about the lawsuit.

**Methods:**
- Social media posts.
- Posts on Reddit and other forums.
- Collaboration with the SuperHero2010 Investigative Unit.

---

### Step 6: Discovery

**Action:** Request documents and data from Devin.

**Key Discovery Targets:**
- Internal communications about token-based system changes.
- Data on user lockouts.
- Marketing materials.
- User data usage policies.

---

### Step 7: Class Certification

**Action:** File a motion to certify the class.

**Requirements:**
- Numerosity: Many users affected.
- Commonality: Common questions of law and fact.
- Typicality: Claims of representative plaintiffs are typical of the class.
- Adequacy: Plaintiffs will fairly and adequately protect the class.

---

### Step 8: Settlement or Trial

**Action:** Negotiate a settlement or proceed to trial.

**Possible Outcomes:**
- Settlement with Devin (compensation, policy changes).
- Trial verdict in favor of plaintiffs.
- Appeal by either party.
