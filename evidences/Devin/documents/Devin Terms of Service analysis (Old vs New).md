# Devin Terms of Service Analysis: [Old](https://cognition.com/platform-terms-of-service-april-2025) vs [New](https://cognition.com/legal/platform-terms-of-service)

- **Document:** Platform Terms of Service (Comparison)
- **Company:** Cognition AI, Inc. (Devin)
- **Versions:** April 3, 2025 & June 30, 2026
- **Analyzed by:** SuperHero2010 Investigative Unit

---

## 1. Overview

I have compared the two versions of Cognition's Terms of Service to identify changes between April 2025 and June 2026. The changes show a clear pattern: **Cognition has tightened its control over user data, expanded its rights, and limited user protections**.

---

## 2. Key Changes Analysis

### 2.1. Data Usage for Model Training: MAJOR CHANGE

| Aspect | April 2025 (Old) | June 2026 (New) |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Model Training Opt-In** | **Opt-in required.** I must explicitly agree to allow my data to be used for training. (Section 3.3.1) | **Opt-out only for paid users.** Cognition may use my data for training unless I am a paid subscriber and actively opt out. (Section 3.3.1) |
| **Free Users** | My data is safe unless I agree. | My data can be used for training automatically. I can't opt out. |

**Analysis:**
This is the most significant change. In April 2025, my data was protected by default, I had to opt in to allow training. In June 2026, the default is reversed: my data can be used for training unless I pay and opt out. This means free users like me have lost control over how our code and inputs are used.

**Relevance to My Experience:**
When I used Devin, I assumed my data was safe. This change reveals that my assumption may no longer be correct. This aligns with my documented concerns about hidden policies and lack of transparency.

**Verdict: Significant Loss of User Rights**

---

### 2.2. Termination Rights: MAJOR CHANGE

| Aspect | April 2025 (Old) | June 2026 (New) |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Termination Without Notice** | Cognition may suspend or terminate my access if I breach the Terms. | Cognition may suspend or terminate my access at any time, **for any reason**, without notice. (Section 7.2) |
| **Refund on Termination** | If Cognition terminates my subscription for any reason other than breach, I receive a pro-rata refund. | Same, but the expanded termination rights make it easier for Cognition to avoid refunds. |

**Analysis:**
The new clause explicitly allows Cognition to terminate my account for any reason, without notice. This is a significant expansion of their power over my access.

**Relevance to My Experience:**
My account was locked with the message "No eligible accounts found", which I now see is fully permitted under the new TOS. I can't challenge it.

**Verdict: Allows Arbitrary Account Termination**

---

### 2.3. Liability Cap: NO CHANGE (Still Unfavorable)

| Aspect | April 2025 (Old) | June 2026 (New) |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Liability Cap** | Capped at amounts paid in the last 6 months. | Capped at the greater of (A) amounts paid in the last 6 months, or (B) $100. (Section 10.2) |

**Analysis:**
The liability cap remains the same. For free users like me, this means I can't claim more than $100 in damages, regardless of the harm caused.

**Relevance to My Experience:**
Even if I suffer significant damage to my project or time, I have no legal recourse beyond $100. This is why I chose to document publicly rather than pursue legal action.

**Verdict: Still Severely Limiting**

---

### 2.4. Indemnification: EXPANDED

| Aspect | April 2025 (Old) | June 2026 (New) |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Cognition's Indemnification** | Applies to all users. | **Applies only to paid users.** Free users aren't covered. (Section 9.1) |
| **My Indemnification** | I must indemnify Cognition for claims arising from my use. | I must indemnify Cognition for claims arising from my use, including claims related to my data. |

**Analysis:**
Cognition has expanded its protection while reducing its obligations to free users. If a third party sues Cognition because of my use of the service, I am responsible for their legal costs.

**Relevance to My Experience:**
This creates a situation where I bear risk for using the service, while Cognition bears none. This is a classic "as-is" approach.

**Verdict: Free Users Have No Protection**

---

### 2.5. Dispute Resolution and Mandatory Arbitration: A Weak Shield for Systemic Deception

**Text (Old - April 2025):** *"The parties will first try in good faith to settle any Dispute within 30 days... If the Dispute is not resolved within 30 days, it shall be resolved by binding arbitration."* (Section 11.1)

**Text (New - June 2026):** *"IN THE EVENT A DISPUTE... THE DISPUTE WILL BE RESOLVED BY BINDING ARBITRATION RATHER THAN IN COURT."* (Section 11.1)

**Analysis:**
- Both the old and new versions contain a mandatory arbitration clause and a class action waiver
- However, this clause **isn't an absolute defense** for Devin. Courts are increasingly unwilling to enforce such waivers when there is strong evidence of **fraud, deceptive trade practices, or violations of public policy**

**My Evidence Undermines This Clause:**
The strength of my documented evidence against Devin (token-based traps, fake errors, deleted messages, account lockouts, and an admission that SWE-1.6 isn't free on the Free plan) suggests that Devin has engaged in **systemic deception**. This is precisely the kind of case where courts may allow class action lawsuits to proceed despite a waiver, as it serves the public interest.

**The Threat to Devin:**
If a class action lawsuit is certified, Devin could face significant penalties, including:
- **Compensatory damages** for users who suffered losses
- **Punitive damages** for intentional misconduct
- **Injunctive relief** forcing changes to their policies (e.g., restoring message-based limits, clarifying data usage)
- **Legal fees** and other costs

**Verdict: The waiver is a defensive tactic, not a guarantee of immunity. My evidence could be the key to overcoming it**

---

### 2.6. Definition Changes: SHIFT IN LANGUAGE

| Aspect | April 2025 (Old) | June 2026 (New) |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Service Tier** | Not defined. | Explicitly defined. (Section 1.6) |
| **Zero Data Retention (ZDR)** | Not defined. | Explicitly defined. (Section 1.8) |
| **Enterprise Customers** | Referenced but not defined. | Explicitly excluded from the TOS. (Section 2.6) |

**Analysis:**
The new TOS introduces definitions for "Service Tier" and "Zero Data Retention", suggesting these concepts are now formalized. This reflects the shift toward a tiered, paid model.

**Relevance to My Experience:**
The introduction of "Service Tier" confirms that my experience as a free user is fundamentally different from paid users.

**Verdict: Formalization of the Tiered System**
