## How to Report

### Step 1: Prepare Your Evidence

| Item | Action |
|------|--------|
| **Your project** | Link to your project on Modrinth *(if still visible)* |
| **Moderation messages** | Screenshots of all communication with Modrinth |
| **Timeline** | Dates of submission, rejection, silence |
| **Content Rules** | Show that no rule supports Modrinth's demands |
| **CurseForge contrast** | If applicable, show that CurseForge accepted your project |

---

### Step 2: Write Your Report (Template)

**Subject:** Formal Complaint: Modrinth, Unfair Moderation and Retaliation

**Body:**

> *Dear Overwolf Team,*
>
> *I am writing to formally report Modrinth for unfair moderation practices and potential retaliation.*
>
> **Summary:**
> - I submitted a project to Modrinth on *[date]*.
> - It was rejected for reasons not found in their Content Rules.
> - Modrinth imposed an indefinite condition: "Your project will remain Withheld until all concerns are addressed", but never specified the concerns.
> - I waited [X] months. No response. No resolution.
> - I published evidence of this process publicly.
> - *[If applicable]* My account was subsequently suspended.
>
> **Evidence:**
> - Full evidence archive: *[Link to my GitHub organization]*
> - Timeline of events: *[Link]*
> - Screenshots: *[Link]*
>
> **Request:**
> I request that Overwolf:
> 1. Investigate Modrinth's moderation practices.
> 2. Clarify whether indefinite conditions with no resolution path are acceptable.
> 3. *[If applicable]* Restore my account.
> 4. Ensure Modrinth adopts fair, transparent moderation.
>
> *Sincerely,*
> *[Your Name/Username]*

---

### Step 3: Send the Report

| Method | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Email** | Send to `partners@overwolf.com` or `support@overwolf.com` |
| **Support Portal** | Use Overwolf's official support channel |
| **Subject** | "Formal Complaint: Modrinth, Unfair Moderation" |

---

### Step 4: Follow Up

| Timing | Action |
|--------|--------|
| 1 week | If no response, send a polite follow-up |
| 2 weeks | If still no response, update your public record |
| 1 month | If ignored, consider public escalation |

## What to Expect

| Overwolf Response | Your Action |
|-------------------|-------------|
| **Investigates** *(Most happened)* | Cooperate fully, provide additional evidence if requested |
| **Asks for more information** *(Likely)* | Respond promptly |
| **Ignores** *(Maybe)* | Update public record, consider other platforms |
| **Intervenes** *(Maybe)* | Accept the outcome, document the resolution |
| **Denies** *(Rare)* | Accept the outcome, update public record |
